Mechanical Bearing Extractor

Design and Structural Validation of a Manual Three-Jaw Bearing Puller

Mechanical Design CAD FEA Structural Analysis

Context

This project marks my first comprehensive mechanical design case study, developed during my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering.

The project involved:

  • Parametric CAD modeling
  • Technical drawings
  • Analytical calculations
  • Finite Element Analysis validation

Project Overview

The objective was to develop a lightweight (~8 kg), manufacturable bearing puller capable of sustaining a 100 kN axial extraction force while maintaining a minimum safety factor of 2.

Design Constraints

  • 100 kN axial load
  • Manual screw actuation
  • Compact geometry
  • Conventional machining
  • Cost-efficient materials

Load Conditions

  • Compression
  • Torsion
  • Bending stress
  • Double shear stress
  • Local contact stress

Material Selection

AISI 1045 Quenched & Tempered Steel was selected due to its:

  • 450 MPa yield strength
  • Good machinability
  • Industrial availability
  • Cost efficiency

Power Screw Design

An M30 square-thread power screw with 2.5 mm pitch was selected to maximize efficiency and reduce manual torque.

Structural Validation

Structural validation was performed using Autodesk Inventor Stress Analysis Environment.

Critical stresses were evaluated in:

  • Spider bending regions
  • Jaw stress concentrations
  • Pin double shear
  • Power screw compression and torsion
