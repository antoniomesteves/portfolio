Mechanical Bearing Extractor

Design and Structural Validation of a Manual Three-Jaw Puller

Mechanical Design Structural Analysis CAD FEA Autodesk Inventor

Context

This project marks my first comprehensive mechanical design case study, developed during my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering. The process included parametric 3D modeling, technical drawings, and structural validation performed using Inventor Stress Analysis Environment.

Project Overview

  • Capacity: 100 kN axial extraction force.
  • Weight: Lightweight (~8 kg) manufacturable solution.
  • Safety: Target Safety Factor (SF) ≥ 2.
  • Tools: Analytical calculations, Material selection, and FEA validation.

Problem / Constraints

  • Manual screw actuation system.
  • Conventional machining processes.
  • Compact and adjustable geometry.
  • Cost-efficient, commercially available materials.

Approach & Engineering Decisions

Material Selection: AISI 1045 QT Steel
Chosen for high yield strength (450 MPa) and excellent machinability. This ensured structural integrity under the 100 kN design load while maintaining cost efficiency.

Power Screw: M30 Square Thread
I specified an M30 power screw with a 2.5mm pitch. Square threads were selected over standard triangular threads to maximize mechanical efficiency and reduce manual torque requirements.
